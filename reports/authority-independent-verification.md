# Independent browser and source verification

The regional researcher responsible for northern New England ran a separate Playwright session against the main page at `http://127.0.0.1:8417/` in desktop Microsoft Edge. The checks below used the map date input, live regional selector, rendered evidence headings, mouse clicks and real browser tabs. The expected transfer dates and titles were supplied independently of the renderer’s calculated active set.

**Results.** All 25 date-and-region assertions passed. Two direct clicks at zoom 6 on March 9, 1665 opened the correct dated polygon popups. All three representative external links loaded their intended historical sources in new tabs on the network-enabled rerun. Edge reported no page exceptions during either successful local map run.
