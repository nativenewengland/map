## Source coverage outside the regional registers

- **Intercolonial grants.** Yale’s Avalon seventeenth-century charter index led to the 1620 New England letters patent, the Rosewell indenture recited in the 1629 Massachusetts Bay charter, the Bradford patent, the Gorges–Mason Maine and Laconia instruments, and the 1664 and 1676 New Jersey conveyances. Broad claims extend beyond the artwork. The New England Council resolved to preserve existing lawful interests when surrendering its charter. The Royal Chancery judgment ending the Massachusetts Bay charter dates to June 1684. [Charter index](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/subject_menus/17th.asp) · [Council declaration](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/17th_century/charter_006.asp) · [USGS Bulletin 226, Massachusetts section](https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/0226/report.pdf)
- **Plymouth and Cape Ann.** The museum’s surviving Peirce indenture establishes the June 1621 allotment. The Council minutes distinguish the subsequent deed poll from the March 1623 disposition of the associates’ complaint. The Sheffield indenture specifies the 500 public acres and settler shares at Cape Ann. Its tract is represented by the existing Gloucester locality. [Pilgrim Hall](https://www.pilgrimhall.org/the-plymouth-patent/) · [Council minutes, pp. 43–45](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ea/Records_of_the_Council_for_New_England_%28IA_recordsofcouncil00coun%29.pdf) · [Sheffield transcription](https://www.colonialsociety.org/node/1054)
- **Narragansett Bay and the Vineyard.** French’s table of manors, p. 49, indexes Sophy and Martin’s Vineyard. The Paine grant and court defense identify Prudence Island. The polygon samples its isolated coastal component in the existing land mask, east of the Chibachuweset label and north of Conanicut. Its end date marks the last court defense cited, with the relinquishment date unresolved. Wightman prints the three Mayhew grants of October 1641 and the 1671 Tisbury manor patent. The 1685 Dongan manor and return conveyance have different reservations. The federal Gay Head historical report identifies the local succession decision and the disputed 1687 conveyance. [Paine instruments, pp. 82–91](https://www.seekingmyroots.com/members/files/G005246A.pdf) · [Wightman, pp. 7–10, 31–33](https://www.seekingmyroots.com/members/files/H003424.pdf) · [Gay Head petition report, pp. 16–17](https://www.bia.gov/sites/default/files/dup/assets/as-ia/ofa/petition/076_wmpghd_MA/076_pf.pdf)
- **Tisbury town and proprietor records.** Reviewed the opening deeds, charter, house lots and meetings in parts 1–4 of the published town book, 1669–1700, and sampled the later returns in parts 5–6. Entries distinguish an earlier sale, grazing rights, enlargement, confirmations, and divisions. Individual corners depend on roads, marked trees and mill-brook calls. The existing Takemmy label supplies the shared locality. Remaining work includes matching undated and retrospective lots on pp. 4–15 and 30–43, and checking Edgartown book I, Dukes deeds and the original town books against their printed recitals. [Published town book](https://ldsgenealogy.com/MA/Records-of-the-town-of-Tisbury-Mass-beginning-June-29-1669-and-ending-May-16-1864.htm)
- **Western edge, New Jersey.** Reviewed the State Archives proprietary chronology and its early-town register, plus Nelson, *Calendar of Records*, East Jersey Patents I, opening folios 1–65, including the group of Carteret confirmations dated 12 May 1668 Old Style. Identifications retain both the name of the grantee and the earlier holder where specified. Exact acreage with illegible fractional type needs confirmation against the patent. The West Jersey settlements on the Delaware are outside the registered lower-Hudson extent. Further review is needed for East Jersey Patents I after folio 65, the later East Jersey registers through 1700, Newark town-book divisions, and underlying survey and warrant books for Bergen, Hackensack, Newark Bay and the Raritan. [State Archives guide](https://www.nj.gov/state/archives/pdf/proprietors.pdf) · [Nelson’s calendar](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/ff/Calendar_of_records_in_the_office_of_the_secretary_of_state_%28IA_calendarofrecord02nels%29.pdf)
- **New York research-room gap.** The State Archives identifies letters patent as series 12943, grantee indexes A4684–A4685 and location indexes A4686–A4687. The guide says these indexes and patents are available on research-room microfilm. Published calendars, digitized individual records and transcriptions allow substantial coverage. A complete reconciliation still requires both indexes and the relevant in-scope register pages. The Rombout certified copy is Columbia item 131. The prior Holwell survey reference and the earlier Wappinger deed remain relevant to the same tract. [NYS Archives guide](https://www.archives.nysed.gov/research/res_tips_011_land_patents.shtml) · [Columbia finding aid](https://findingaids.library.columbia.edu/pdf/cul-4079215.pdf) · [Dutchess County, Holwell survey context](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Graphics/Transformations_of_an_American_County_1683-1983_ocr.pdf)
