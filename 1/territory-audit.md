## Coverage questions

- **Ottawa, St. Lawrence and Wabanaki country** — Stadacona and Hochelaga are recorded in the 1530s. Champlain's 1603 journey does not establish them as continuing seats. Political affiliations in the northern interior remain unresolved. [Source](https://ontarioarchaeology.org/wp-content/uploads/OA_web_100.pdf)
- **Great Lakes and northern New York** — The Deer nation's admission to the Wendat confederacy is usually placed around 1610. The reconstruction distinguishes that later membership. Some western seats are beyond the terrain frame. [Source](https://www.asau.ru/files/pdf/2036197.pdf)
- **Southern New England and Long Island** — The Hassanamisco history also identifies Pegan and Nipnet. Their separate seats and jurisdiction around 1600 require further evidence. [Source](https://nipmucnation.org/history)
- **Southern New England and Long Island** — Nantucket accounts identify Wanackmamack, Nicornoose, Autopscot and Potconet in connection with 1659. The distribution of authority among their predecessors in 1600 remains open. [Source](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d4/Nantucket%3B_a_history_%28IA_nantuckethistory01doug%29.pdf)
- **Hudson, Delaware and Susquehanna valleys** — Kechemeckes, Little Siconese, Asomoches, Armewamese, Axion, Mosilian, Sewapois, Narraticons, Okanickon and Minguannan also occur in later records. Their circa-1600 seats and relationships have yet to be established here. [Source](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Gazetteer/Places/America/United_States/_Topics/history/_Texts/WESDDV/5*.html)
- **Hudson, Delaware and Susquehanna valleys** — The archaeological labels Shenks Ferry and Monongahela do not establish a single polity for each settlement area. The western and central Pennsylvania interior remains an evidence gap. [Source](https://npshistory.com/publications/choh/aie-v1-2011.pdf)
- **Chesapeake, Delmarva and Virginia Piedmont** — The Monacan Nation records Tauxnitanias, Shackaconias, Outponcas, Tegoneaes, Whonkentyaes, Stegarakes and Hassinnungas in the 1608 account of Mannahoac tribute. Individual seats await defensible placement. [Source](https://www.monacannation.gov/step-back-in-time.html)
- **Chesapeake, Delmarva and Virginia Piedmont** — The 1607 reconstruction identifies 28–32 groups under Wahunsenacawh. Membership changed through conquest and diplomacy. The map distinguishes the six inherited districts from later evidence. [Source](https://encyclopediavirginia.org/entries/powhatan-d-1618/)
- **Chesapeake, Delmarva and Virginia Piedmont** — Opiscopank, lower and upper Cuttatawomen, Pissaseck, Nantaughtacund and Onawmanient are individually named in the 1608 voyage region. Local placements and political affiliations at the target date remain under review. [Source](https://www.nps.gov/cajo/getinvolved/upload/SignificanceStatement-2.pdf)
- **Delmarva · researched, extent unresolved** — Tockwogh and Ozinies/Wicomiss: the NPS distinguishes plausible town sites, a river association, and contested land. Which areas between the Chester and Sassafras were governed by each polity in 1600? [Source](https://www.nps.gov/cajo/learn/upload/CAJO_FINAL-ICL-REPORT_2023_0314-508.pdf)
- **Southern Delmarva · researched, authority unresolved** — Accomack and Accohannock have separately documented cores. The strength of their political relationship at the target date and the northern reach toward Pocomoke remain open. [Source](https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/pdf_files/AC-081_Historic_AH_Survey_Eastern_Shore_2017_CRAI_report.pdf)
- **St. Lawrence · researched, seasonal use unresolved** — Iroquet met allies near the Richelieu but wintered upriver. The meeting place cannot supply the location or extent of a homeland. [Source](https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/iroquet_1F.html)
- **Western Long Island and lower Hudson · researched, extent unresolved** — Canarsee, Tappan and Wiechquaeskeck appear in later records. Which contact-period land-use areas correspond to political jurisdiction before colonial land transactions? [Source](https://www.nan.usace.army.mil/Portals/37/Appendix%20A8_Tier%201%20Cultural%20Resources_SEP2022.pdf)
- **Northern and western sheet margins · further research needed** — Nipissirini, Atikamekw family territories and western Erie communities have regional locators. A systematic review of neighboring political jurisdictions beyond these locators remains outstanding. [Source](https://new-wordpress.algonquinnation.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/algonquin-history-james-morrison-sicani-oct-2006-revisedpublic.pdf)
- **Middle Eastern Shore · researched, earlier affiliation unresolved** — A 1669 act locates Choptank land on the south bank. It postdates the target by nearly seventy years. Distinct Choptank government and its relationship to Kuskarawock in 1600 cannot be reconstructed from that tract. [Source](https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/speccolm/m3000/m3100/m3173/pdf/m3173-1777.pdf)
- **Georgian Bay · researched, internal division unresolved** — Bear and Cord were founding Wendat nations. The Penetanguishene assessment places its study site in Bear territory, but assigning the intervening shores to Bear, Cord or Rock around 1600 requires contemporary settlement sequences. [Source](https://penetanguishene.ca/media/zwqkxsa1/2-stage-1-and-2-archaeological-assessment-august-9-2022-138-rse.pdf)
